Kitchen Hot Water

Restaurant and Food Service Water Heaters

In a restaurant, the dish machine, the sinks, and the handwashing stations all pull from the same supply, and a heater that's undersized or failing reveals itself as lukewarm water at the dish machine and a backed-up dish room. A high-temp dish machine typically needs final rinse water near 180°F, which usually means a booster heater working alongside the main supply. We handle high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and sizing for peak demand, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Many restaurant inspection problems trace back to water temperature. If the main heater can't keep up, handwashing sinks go lukewarm, the three-compartment sink falls below temperature, and the dish machine can't hit its final rinse during the rush. Our technicians verify temperatures from the tank all the way to the fixtures and document every visit in written reports that inspectors and landlords can review.

Replacements and maintenance visits are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits the hours you're open. If a job can't be done during service, we install overnight or before opening, so hot water is ready when the first shift arrives.

Safe Installation

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can lead to carbon monoxide hazards from bad venting, scalding at the tap, Legionella growth in water stored too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Williamsburg Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas piping sizing, relief valve discharge piping, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where required, and permits and inspections. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test dates, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms.
